**High-value fields we receive but the app does not store** (candidates for new columns)

- Both: MLS number / listing id (`mlsId.value`, `source.listing_id`), MLS name (`source.name`), raw MLS status, price per sqft, HOA fee (`hoa.value` / `hoa.fee`), full/half baths, stories, garage (`skGarageSpaces`, `description.garage`), days on market (`dom`), price reductions (`price_reduced_amount`, `last_price_change_*`), open houses, virtual/3D tour flags, listing tags/key facts, new-construction flag, last update timestamps.
- Realtor only: county FIPS (`location.county.fips_code`, 100%), AVM estimates (`estimate`, `current_estimates[]` with high/low and provider), tax-record ids (`tax_record.public_record_id`, `cl_id`), agent/office/broker names, emails, phones (`advertisers[]`, `source.agents[]`), builder/community for new construction, pet policy, structured detail categories (`details[]` - interior/exterior/HOA text), rental units (`units[]` price/beds/baths/sqft per floor plan), street-view URL, `other_listings`.

**Not in any search response** (need detail pages or other sources): deposit (sale), owner, full tax history, price/sale history, school data, full photo lists for Redfin, Redfin agent names for non-Redfin listings.
